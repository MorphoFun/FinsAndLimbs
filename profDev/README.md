**Applying for the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)**
- [NSF GRFP:](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/grfp) solicitation for the program; start here first!  
- [NSF GRFP - Additional resources:](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/grfp/applicants/resources) essay templates, tips for competitive applications and letters of reference, PDF of an on overview of the program, etc.   
- [NSF's Application Help Videos:](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/grfp/applicants/application-help-videos) tips on formatting your application, avoiding common mistakes, understanding how your applications will be reviewed, etc.    
- [Alex Lang’s NSF fellowship advice:](https://www.alexhunterlang.com/nsf-fellowship) general info and advice about the GRFP, tips on preparing your statements, example essays, etc.   
- [NSF GRF Help website:](https://grfphelp.github.io/) Example essays and general advice for NSF GRFP Applications. Not affiliated with the NSF.   
- [Example GRFP essays for Evolution and Ecology:](https://github.com/ybrandvain/GRFP) personal and research statements, and even some copies of the reviewers' comments!
